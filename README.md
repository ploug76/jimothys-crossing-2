# Jimothy's Crossing 2: Market Mayhem

The 16-bit sequel to Jimothy's Crossing. Jimothy the raccoon heads for Pike Place Market and the Ballard Locks to build five new trash-can dens.

Play: https://ploug76.github.io/jimothys-crossing-2/

New in this game:
- New setting: Pike Place Market, the waterfront boardwalk, the Locks, the Great Wheel and the Olympic Mountains.
- New hazards: flying-fish lanes, delivery vans, fish carts, forklifts, tour buses and skateboarders.
- New mechanics: a tide that pushes rafts back, sinking kayaks and buoys, sea-lion rafts, and traffic lights at the boardwalk crossing.
- A short story intro that picks up where the first game ended, new chiptune music, and a gentle difficulty ramp.

Free to play, no locks. Arrows, WASD or swipe to move. P pauses, M mutes.
Single-file HTML, built with `./build.sh` from `src/`. It works offline as a PWA.
